## Project Overview
This Tableau project delivers a comprehensive analytical solution for Global Retail Pvt Ltd, enabling leadership to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and operational efficiency through interactive dashboards. Using the company’s Orders, Customers, Products, and Sales Targets datasets, the analysis integrates multiple data sources through joins, relationships, and blending to create a unified view of business performance.

The Sales Overview dashboard highlights monthly and yearly sales trends, regional performance through filled maps, category contribution, cumulative running totals, discount–profit relationships and sales distribution using histograms. It equips management to identify seasonal patterns, high‑performing categories and sub categories and profitability risks.

The Product & Customer Insights dashboard focuses on order seasonality, top‑performing products in terms of profit, quantity sold and margin, dynamic Top‑N product selection, customer segmentation and identification of the most valuable customers using sets. Shipping Days calculation supports deeper operational insights.

Interactive features—including filter actions, highlight actions, navigation buttons, and web page actions—ensure a seamless user experience. Together, these dashboards provide actionable insights that help management optimize pricing, improve regional strategies, enhance customer targeting, and strengthen product portfolio decisions.
## Business Context
Global Retail Pvt Ltd sells furniture, office supplies and technology products across multiple regions. The leadership team wants an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyse sales performance, customer behaviour and product profitability. As a data analyst, you are required to analyse the provided dataset and build interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.
## Client Expectation
Build interactive Tableau dashboards that help management understand *sales trends*, *regional performance*, *customer behaviour* and *product profitability*.
## Analytical Requirements
1. Management wants to understand how sales are changing over time. Create a visualization that shows the sales trend by month and year and identify seasonal patterns.
2. The leadership team wants to know which regions generate the highest sales and profit. Create a geographical visualization to compare regional performance.
3. The product team wants to understand category performance. Show how each product category contributes to total sales.
4. . Identify which sub-categories are the best performing within each category.
5. The marketing team wants insights about customer segments. Show how sales are distributed across different customer segments.
6. Management suspects that high discounts may reduce profitability. Build a visualization that shows the relationship between discount and profit.
7. The company wants to identify its best-performing products. Create a report showing the top performing products based on sales.
8. Management wants flexibility to dynamically view Top N products. Create a parameter that allows users to select the number of top products to display.
9. The finance team wants to understand how sales values are distributed across different ranges. Create a histogram using sales bins.
10. The operations team wants to monitor shipping efficiency. Calculate the number of shipping days between order date and ship date and analyze which regions take longer to ship products.
11. The business team wants to identify the most valuable customers. Show the top customers based on total sales.
12. Create a set that separates Top Customers from Other Customers.
13. Management wants to compare actual sales with regional sales targets. Use the sales targets dataset and perform data blending to compare actual sales vs target.
14. Combine Orders and Customers tables using joins so that customer details are available in the analysis.
15. Connect Products data using relationships so product information can be used in the dashboard.
16. Create calculated fields such as Profit Ratio and Shipping Days to support business analysis.
17. Create groups to combine related sub-categories into broader product groups.
18. Apply filters so that users can filter the dashboard by Region, Category, Segment, and Year.
19. Create a running total of sales using table calculations to show cumulative performance over time.
20. Calculate Percent of Total Sales using table calculations to understand category contribution.
21. Create two dashboards: one for Sales Overview and another for Product & Customer Insights.
22. The dashboards must include multiple visualizations such as line charts, bar charts, maps, scatter plots, histograms, and tree maps.
23. Create a filter action so that when users click on a category, all other visuals update accordingly.
24. Create a highlight action so that when users hover over a region or category, related data points are highlighted.
25. Add navigation buttons that allow users to move easily between dashboards.
26. Create a web page action so that clicking on a product category opens a related company website.
27. Ensure the dashboards are interactive, easy to read, and visually appealing for business users.
28. Provide your insights to help management make decisions.

## Key Insights
* Monthly sales trend is consistent over the years except for a steep decline in December 2024.
* Highest sales occur in November 2024, just before the sharp fall.
* All three categories contribute  to sales almost equally (35, 33 and 32% each); Technology being the highest contributor.
* South generates highest revenue and profit.
* Maharashtra is the highest revenue generating state.
* Higher discounts tend to reduce profitability. Keep discounts below 11%.
* Tables, Paper and Accessories are the highest revenue generating sub categories.
* Most orders have sales in the range of 400-450.
* The most revenue generating customer segment is Consumer, followed by Home Office.
* March and November are the busiest months in terms of Orders placed.
* Customer 75 and Customer 147 are the top two customers in terms of revenue, each having more than 8k of total order value.
* East Region has a higher Shipping Days Average (4-5 days) compared to the other three (3-4 days).
* Large items take more time to be shipped than Small items.
* Paper is the most profitable sub category with the highest profit ratio.
* The highest number of orders made by an individual customer is 13.
